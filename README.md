
Sehat-AI: Sovereign Voice-First Medical Assistant for Bharat

Tagline: Healthcare That Speaks Your Language
Built for: The Great Indian AI Internship Challenge | Tech Stack: Gnani AI (Prisma v2.5, Timbre v2.5) & Gemini 2.5 Flash

1. The Problem

• For Rural & Elderly Patients: Millions of patients in Tier-2/3 cities and villages walk out of busy government or private hospitals with handwritten English prescriptions and complex lab reports they cannot read. Consequently, they forget medicine schedules, take wrong pills, or panic over medical jargon.
• For Overworked Indian Doctors: With heavy OPD patient loads, doctors do not have time to sit and type detailed prescriptions or explain every lab parameter in local dialects.

2. Our Solution: Sehat-AI (Zero-Reading Healthcare)

Sehat-AI is a mobile-first, voice-driven healthcare companion designed so elderly patients, rural families, and ASHA workers need zero reading or typing skills.
• Hands-Free Hinglish OPD (Doctor Mode): Doctors speak naturally in noisy clinic environments using code-mixed Hinglish (e.g., "Subah-shaam khane ke baad Paracetamol 500mg teen din tak"). Sehat-AI converts speech into a structured digital prescription in seconds.
• Native Voice Audio Guide (10+ Indian Languages): Automatically translates medical abbreviations (BD, TDS, PC) into warm, polite audio instructions in the patient's native tongue (Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Kannada, etc.).
• Interactive 3D Human Body Lab Report & Symptom Explainer: Patients upload a blood test report or speak their pain (e.g., "Pain in the right side of my stomach"). Sehat-AI maps the affected organs onto an Interactive 3D Human Body with color-coded pins (Green = Normal, Red = Attention Needed) and explains every test value calmly in simple native audio.
• Live Pill Strip Scanner & Safety Guard: Elderly users scan any medicine strip with their phone camera and ask, "What is this medicine for?" Sehat-AI identifies the pill and verifies whether it matches the doctor's active prescription.
• My Care Circle (Family & ASHA Escalation): Stores emergency contacts (family members, relatives, local ASHA workers) with live GPS location to trigger instant WhatsApp and voice alerts if a dose is missed or critical lab values appear.

3. How I Built It on Gnani AI's Sovereign Stack

• Gnani Prisma v2.5 (Speech-to-Text): Powers real-time, noise-resilient transcription across 10+ Indian languages and Hinglish code-mixing inside busy hospital OPDs.
• Gnani Timbre v2.5 (Text-to-Speech): Delivers natural, empathetic Indian voices with accurate pronunciation of Indian names, dosages, and vernacular medical advice.
• Multimodal Medical Reasoning Engine: Extracts structured prescription JSON, analyzes uploaded blood test images, maps 3D anatomical hotspots, and verifies pill strips with strict medical safety disclaimers.
